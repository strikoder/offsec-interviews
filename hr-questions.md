# HR Questions

Some of these HR questions overlap with technical topics.  
There are no absolute right answers, most responses here reflect how I would personally answer them, while others are adapted or collected from various sources (including online articles/ blogs and AI material).

## Background and Motivation
- **Why did you choose/shift a career in/into cybersecurity?**  
  Answer: Because I enjoy problem-solving, understanding systems deeply, and breaking things to learn how to defend them.

- **What area of cybersecurity interests you the most?**  
  Answer: Offensive security/ Red teaming especially Active Directory attacks.


## Work Ethic and Mindset
- **How do you handle failure (during an assessment)?**  
  Answer: I review my steps, document what didn’t work, and adjust my approach instead of repeating the same path.

- **What’s your approach when you find a critical vulnerability?**  
  Answer: Document impact, proof of concept, and mitigation clearly before reporting it responsibly.

## Teamwork and Communication
- **How do you explain a technical issue to a non-technical client?**  
  Answer: I translate risk into business terms by focusing on impact, not just CVSS or exploit details.

- **Have you ever disagreed with a teammate’s finding? How did you handle it?**  
  Answer: I verify the claim objectively, share evidence, and discuss calmly until consensus is reached.

## Ethics and Professional Conduct
- **What would you do if you gained access beyond the defined scope?**  
  Answer: Stop immediately, document the event, and notify the client or project lead.


## Career Goals
- **Where do you see yourself in the next 3–5 years?**  
  Answer: Leading red team engagements and contributing to developing internal security testing tools.

- **Which certifications or skills are you planning to pursue next?**  
  Answer: CRTP or CRTO for offensive security depth, and better scripting for automation.
